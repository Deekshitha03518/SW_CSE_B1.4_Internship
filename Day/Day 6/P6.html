Position Property: Dictates how an element is placed on a webpage. Its controls whether an element stays in the normal page flow, or moves relative to its neighbours or locks it in a place when user scrolls 1.Static: Normal page flow. (Default) 2.Relative: Moves away from its normal spot without disturbuing the neighbours. 3.Absolute: Files out of normal flow,positioning itself inside its closet relative parent 4.Fixed: Looks to the viewport. Stays in the exact same spot on the screen while scrolling. 5.Sticky: Scrolls naturally with the page, then looks in palce like FIXED when hits the edge.

Both display property and position property,controls layput,difference is that display creates the box and position move the box.
